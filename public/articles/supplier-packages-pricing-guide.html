Supplier Packages and Pricing Guide

The cheapest package is not always the best value. Compare what is included, what is excluded and what happens if the plan changes.

📚 8 min read • Updated June 2026

In this guide
  1. Compare like for like
  2. Understand POA pricing
  3. Deposits and payment schedules
  4. Clearer supplier packages

Compare like for like

Two packages with similar headline prices can include very different things. Check hours, travel, setup time, staffing, equipment, VAT, delivery, collection, overtime and any editing or post-event work.

When comparing suppliers, write the package inclusions side by side rather than only comparing the total price.

Understand POA pricing

POA is not automatically a problem. It can be sensible where the price depends on date, venue, travel, guest count or scope. However, the profile should still explain how pricing is built and what information is needed for a useful quote.

Check deposits and payment schedules

Before booking, confirm the deposit amount, balance due date, cancellation wording, postponement options and whether payments are refundable. Keep everything in writing and avoid paying until the scope is clear.

Build clearer packages as a supplier

Clear packages reduce enquiry friction. State who the package suits, what is included, what is not included and what common upgrades cost. Customers are more likely to enquire when they understand the starting point.
