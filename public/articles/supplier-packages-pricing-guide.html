Supplier Packages and Pricing Guide

The cheapest package is not always the best value. Compare what is included, what is excluded and what happens if the plan changes.

EventFlow Team Published: 14 June 2026 3 min read Intermediate
0% Read

  • Build clearer packages as a supplier

The short version

  • Compare package inclusions side by side — hours, travel, setup time, staffing, equipment, VAT, delivery, collection, overtime and any editing or post-event work — rather than only comparing the total price.
  • POA pricing is not automatically a problem: it can be sensible where price depends on date, venue, travel, guest count or scope, provided the supplier explains how the quote is built.
  • Before booking, confirm the deposit amount, balance due date, cancellation wording, postponement options and whether payments are refundable — and keep it all in writing.
  • Suppliers reduce enquiry friction by stating who a package suits, what is included, what is excluded, and what common upgrades cost.

Compare like for like

Two packages with similar headline prices can include very different things. Before you compare totals, check what each one actually covers:

  • Hours booked, including setup and pack-down time
  • Travel
  • Staffing
  • Equipment
  • VAT
  • Delivery and collection
  • Overtime
  • Any editing or post-event work

Write inclusions side by side. When comparing suppliers, write the package inclusions side by side rather than only comparing the total price.

Understand POA pricing

POA (price on application) is not automatically a problem. It can be sensible where the price genuinely depends on:

  • Date
  • Venue
  • Travel
  • Guest count
  • Scope

However, the profile should still explain how pricing is built and what information is needed for a useful quote. A POA listing that sets out its pricing logic is doing its job; one that gives you nothing to go on is not.

Check deposits and payment schedules

Before booking, confirm the following:

  • The deposit amount
  • The balance due date
  • Cancellation wording
  • Postponement options
  • Whether payments are refundable

Get it in writing. Keep everything in writing and avoid paying until the scope is clear.

Build clearer packages as a supplier

Clear packages reduce enquiry friction. A strong package listing states:

  • Who the package suits
  • What is included
  • What is not included
  • What common upgrades cost

Customers are more likely to enquire when they understand the starting point.
