Wedding Anniversary Gifts by Year

The traditional and modern UK gift for every wedding anniversary milestone, from the 1st to the 60th.

The short version

  • The UK follows a traditional gift list (cotton, paper, wood…) alongside a more flexible modern list — there's no rule that says you have to pick one over the other.
  • The best-known milestones are the 1st, 5th, 10th, 25th (silver), 30th (pearl), 40th (ruby), 50th (gold) and 60th (diamond).
  • Modern lists tend to suit gift-buying better (silverware, watches, jewellery); traditional materials suit a theme, a handmade gift or a keepsake.
  • Whichever list you use, a personal touch — engraving, a photo, a shared memory — matters more than matching the material exactly.

The full list, year by year

The nine milestone years

These are the anniversaries UK couples mark most — the ones worth planning a proper gift, card or celebration around.

1st anniversary: Cotton (traditional) / Clocks (modern)

Cotton reflects a marriage still being "woven together" — practical, everyday gifts like personalised bedding, towels or a cotton shirt work well. The modern clock theme suits an engraved mantel clock or watch marking "time together" from the first year.

5th anniversary: Wood (traditional) / Silverware (modern)

Wood symbolises a marriage strong enough to weather its first real tests. Popular gifts include engraved wooden photo frames, chopping boards or furniture. Silverware — a photo frame, cutlery set or jewellery box — is the modern equivalent.

10th anniversary: Tin/Aluminium (traditional) / Diamond jewellery (modern)

Tin represents flexibility and durability. Traditional gifts include tin-plated keepsakes or aluminium homeware. Most UK couples now lean modern at 10 years — a diamond pendant, earrings or a ring upgrade is the most popular choice.

20th anniversary: China (traditional) / Platinum (modern)

China marks two decades of a marriage that can be beautiful yet needs care — fine china, a commissioned piece of pottery or a china anniversary plate all suit the theme. Platinum jewellery is the modern alternative.

25th anniversary: Silver

The silver wedding anniversary is one of the most widely celebrated UK milestones, and traditional and modern lists agree on silver. Popular gifts include silver jewellery, a silver photo frame, engraved silverware or a silver-themed party.

30th anniversary: Pearl (traditional) / Diamond (modern)

Pearls symbolise a marriage that has matured into something valuable and rare. Pearl jewellery is the classic traditional gift, while many couples now mark 30 years with a diamond piece instead.

40th anniversary: Ruby

The ruby wedding anniversary — another milestone where the traditional and modern lists agree. Ruby jewellery is the obvious gift, but a ruby-red themed celebration, a bottle of red wine from the couple's wedding year, or a ruby-accented keepsake all work.

50th anniversary: Gold

The golden wedding anniversary is the milestone most UK couples build a proper celebration around — often a family party rather than just a gift. Gold jewellery, a gold-engraved keepsake or a golden-themed renewal of vows are all traditional choices.

60th anniversary: Diamond

The diamond wedding anniversary marks 60 years of marriage and is treated as one of the most significant milestones a couple can reach. Diamond jewellery is the standard gift, alongside a family celebration marking the achievement itself.

Not every year needs a themed gift. Many couples only follow the list for the well-known milestones (1st, 5th, 10th, 25th, 30th, 40th, 50th, 60th) and simply mark the years in between with a card, a dinner out or a small treat.

Choosing the right gift

Once you know the theme for the year, the material is really just a starting point. What tends to matter more:

  • Personalise it: engraving, a shared photo, a meaningful date or a private joke turns a generic gift into a keepsake.
  • Consider an experience: a weekend away, a meal at the restaurant of your first date, or tickets to something you both love can matter more than an object.
  • Match your budget to the milestone: smaller, more frequent years don't need an expensive gift — save the bigger spend for the milestones that mean the most to you both.
  • Match the gift to the couple, not just the year: a couple who prize experiences over objects, or vice versa, should feel free to ignore the theme entirely.

Common questions

What is the traditional gift for a 30th wedding anniversary?

The traditional 30th wedding anniversary gift is pearl, symbolising a marriage that has matured into something rare and valuable. The modern equivalent is diamond, which many UK couples now choose instead.

What are the wedding anniversary gifts by year?

The traditional UK list runs from cotton (1st) through wood (5th), tin (10th), china (20th), silver (25th), pearl (30th), ruby (40th), gold (50th) and diamond (60th), with a separate modern list offering an alternative gift for each year.

Is there a modern alternative to traditional anniversary gifts?

Yes — alongside the traditional material-based list, a modern list offers a different suggested gift for each year, such as diamond jewellery for a 10th anniversary instead of tin. Neither list is "correct"; couples are free to follow either, both, or neither.
