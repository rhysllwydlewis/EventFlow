UK Wedding Cost Guide 2026

What weddings really cost in the UK in 2026 — venue, catering, photography, cake and entertainment, broken down by item.

The short version

  • UK wedding costs vary enormously by region, guest count and how much of the day is DIY — treat every figure here as a starting range, not a quote.
  • Venue and catering are usually the two biggest line items, typically together making up 40–50% of the total budget.
  • Photography, videography, entertainment and hair & makeup are the categories with the widest price spread — a small deposit gets you a very different service to a full-day, multi-supplier booking.
  • Guest count is the single biggest lever on total cost: cutting the guest list affects venue, catering, stationery and favours all at once.

How Much Does Each Thing Cost?

The most common cost questions couples search for, answered directly.

How much does the average wedding cost in the UK?

Most full UK weddings (venue, catering, photography, attire, entertainment and extras for 60–100 guests) land somewhere between £15,000 and £25,000, though it's entirely possible to spend far less with a smaller guest list, a weekday date or a DIY approach — or considerably more at a premium venue. Treat any single "average" figure with caution; the spread between a modest and a lavish wedding is enormous.

How much does a wedding cake cost?

Most UK wedding cakes cost between £200 and £600, depending on the number of tiers, design detail and whether it needs to cater for allergies or dietary requirements. A simple two-tier cake sits at the lower end; an elaborate multi-tier design with sugar flowers or intricate icing work costs more.

How much does a wedding DJ or band cost?

A DJ typically costs £400–£800 for an evening reception, while a live band usually runs £800–£1,800 depending on the number of musicians and set length. Get quotes for the exact hours and equipment you need — a longer set or extra speakers/lighting adds to the price.

How much do wedding photos cost?

Wedding photography in the UK typically costs £800–£2,500. The main variables are how many hours of coverage you book, whether there's a second photographer, and whether an album or prints are included in the package rather than sold separately.

How much is wedding insurance?

Wedding insurance typically costs £25–£75 for standard cover, rising for higher-value weddings or added extras like marquee or honeymoon cover. It's a small fraction of total spend against protection for supplier failure, cancellation or postponement.

How much does hair and makeup cost for a wedding?

Bridal hair and makeup typically costs £150–£450, depending on whether a trial is included and how many other people (bridesmaids, mothers) are also having hair and makeup done on the day.

How much does a wedding planner cost?

Full wedding planning services typically cost £800–£3,000 or more, while on-the-day coordination only — someone to run the day itself rather than plan it from scratch — usually costs considerably less.

How much should you give as a wedding gift?

There's no fixed rule, but a common UK guide is around £30–£50 per guest for a colleague or acquaintance, £50–£100 for a friend, and £100+ for close family — adjusted for whether you're attending as a couple and contributing to a shared gift.

Ways to Save Money

  • Move the date: a Friday, Sunday or weekday wedding, or an off-peak month (November–March), is typically significantly cheaper than a Saturday in peak season.
  • Trim the guest list: every guest adds to catering, favours, stationery and often venue minimum spend — cutting numbers is the single biggest lever on total cost.
  • Choose a venue with more included: in-house catering, furniture and decor bundled into the hire fee often works out cheaper than an empty-shell venue plus separate suppliers.
  • Buy second-hand or pre-loved: dresses, decor and unused stationery can be found well below retail — see EventFlow's second-hand wedding items guide.
  • Simplify flowers and favours: in-season, locally sourced flowers cost less than imported or out-of-season blooms, and inexpensive favours (under £1–3 per guest) are just as well received as elaborate ones.
  • Build a contingency fund anyway: even a tight budget should set aside 10% for unexpected costs, so a surprise expense doesn't derail the whole plan.

Common questions

How much does the average wedding cost in the UK?

Most full UK weddings for 60–100 guests cost somewhere between £15,000 and £25,000, though it's entirely possible to spend far less with a smaller guest list or DIY approach, or considerably more at a premium venue.

What is usually the most expensive part of a wedding?

Venue hire and catering are typically the two largest line items, together often making up 40–50% of the total budget, followed by photography, entertainment and attire.

How can I plan a wedding on a smaller budget?

Trim the guest list first — it reduces catering, stationery and favour costs all at once — then move to an off-peak date, choose a venue with more included as standard, and consider second-hand items for dress, decor and stationery.
