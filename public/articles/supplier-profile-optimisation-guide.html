Supplier Profile Guide: How to Get More Enquiries on EventFlow

A strong supplier profile helps customers understand what you do, why you are credible and what to do next.

📚 9 min read • Updated June 2026

In this guide
  1. Make the first screen count
  2. Write for the customer
  3. Reduce enquiry friction
  4. Maintain it monthly

Make the first screen count

Customers decide quickly whether to keep reading. Use a clear profile image, a straightforward headline, your service area and the type of events you are best suited to. Avoid making customers work out what you actually provide.

Write for the customer

Your profile copy should answer customer questions, not just describe your business. Explain what is included, how bookings work, what information you need for a quote and what makes your service different.

  • Use plain language and short paragraphs.
  • Show real event types, not vague claims.
  • Explain pricing or say clearly when pricing is bespoke.
  • Add trust signals such as reviews, insurance and experience.

Reduce enquiry friction

Make it easy for customers to contact you. If you need date, location, guest count and event type before quoting, say so. Clear expectations improve enquiry quality and reduce back-and-forth messages.

Maintain the profile monthly

Review your profile at least monthly. Update photos, remove unavailable packages, check links and refresh seasonal wording. A profile that looks current feels safer to enquire with.

Improve your supplier presence

Review your EventFlow dashboard and update the areas customers rely on most.
