Supplier Profile Guide: How to Get More Enquiries on EventFlow

A strong supplier profile helps customers understand what you do, why you are credible and what to do next.

EventFlow Team Published: 14 June 2026 3 min read Intermediate
0% Read

The short version

  • Customers decide quickly whether to keep reading — lead with a clear profile image, a straightforward headline, your service area and the event types you suit best.
  • Write profile copy that answers customer questions: what's included, how bookings work, what you need for a quote, and what makes your service different.
  • Add trust signals such as reviews, insurance and experience, and either state pricing clearly or say plainly when it's bespoke.
  • List what you need before quoting — date, location, guest count and event type — to improve enquiry quality and cut back-and-forth messages.
  • Review your profile at least monthly: update photos, remove unavailable packages, check links and refresh seasonal wording.

Make the First Screen Count

Customers decide quickly whether to keep reading. Use a clear profile image, a straightforward headline, your service area and the type of events you are best suited to. Avoid making customers work out what you actually provide.

  • A clear profile image: the first thing a customer sees, and the first thing that builds — or costs you — trust.
  • A straightforward headline: say plainly what you do rather than leaving it to be inferred.
  • Your service area: so customers can tell in seconds whether you cover their event.
  • The type of events you are best suited to: so customers can judge fit before they read any further.

Don't make customers guess. Avoid making customers work out what you actually provide — if the first screen leaves that unclear, most will move on before reading further.

Write for the Customer

Your profile copy should answer customer questions, not just describe your business. Explain what is included, how bookings work, what information you need for a quote and what makes your service different.

  • Use plain language and short paragraphs.
  • Show real event types, not vague claims.
  • Explain pricing or say clearly when pricing is bespoke.
  • Add trust signals such as reviews, insurance and experience.

Reduce Enquiry Friction

Make it easy for customers to contact you. If you need date, location, guest count and event type before quoting, say so. Clear expectations improve enquiry quality and reduce back-and-forth messages.

Say what you need upfront. List the date, location, guest count and event type you need before you can quote — clear expectations improve enquiry quality and reduce back-and-forth messages.

Maintain the Profile Monthly

Review your profile at least monthly. Update photos, remove unavailable packages, check links and refresh seasonal wording. A profile that looks current feels safer to enquire with.

  • Update photos.
  • Remove unavailable packages.
  • Check links.
  • Refresh seasonal wording.
