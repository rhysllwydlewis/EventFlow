Wedding Fayres and Public Events Calendar Guide

Wedding fayres and open days are useful when you treat them as planning sessions, not just inspiration trips.

📚 7 min read • Updated June 2026

In this guide
  1. What couples should look for
  2. How to prepare
  3. How suppliers should use public events
  4. What to do afterwards

What to look for as a couple

Use public events to compare suppliers in context. Watch how they explain their service, how quickly they answer questions and whether their style suits your event. Take notes on price ranges, availability, package options and any follow-up they promise.

How to prepare before you go

Go with your date, rough guest count, venue type and budget range. You do not need every answer, but suppliers can give better guidance if they understand the shape of your event.

  • Prepare a shortlist of must-see suppliers.
  • Take photos of displays and business cards.
  • Ask what is included, not just the headline price.
  • Check availability before getting attached.

How suppliers should list public events

For suppliers, a public calendar listing should be clear, dated and useful. Include who the event is for, what customers can see, whether booking is needed, parking details and what makes the event worth attending.

Use EventFlow after the event

After a fayre, turn your notes into action. Save suppliers, compare packages and update your plan while the details are still fresh. Avoid booking on pressure alone; a good supplier will be happy for you to review the details properly.
