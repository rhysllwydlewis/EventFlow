Event Licences and Legal Basics in the UK

Some events need permissions before they can go ahead. Check early, because licences, notices and local authority requirements can take time.

📚 10 min read • Updated June 2026

Important: This is practical planning information, not legal advice. Rules can change and local requirements vary, so check with the relevant local authority and official guidance before relying on a licence position.

In this guide
  1. Temporary Event Notices
  2. Food and catering
  3. Music and entertainment
  4. Early checklist

Temporary Event Notices

A Temporary Event Notice may be needed for certain short-term events involving licensable activities such as alcohol sales, regulated entertainment or late-night refreshment. The exact position depends on the event, venue and activity.

Start with the GOV.UK guidance, then check the local council process for the event location: GOV.UK Temporary Event Notice guidance.

Food and catering registration

If food is being prepared, stored, handled or supplied as part of a business activity, food registration and hygiene duties may apply. Professional caterers should be able to explain their registration, hygiene rating position and allergen controls.

Check current Food Standards Agency guidance: Register a food business.

Music, entertainment and noise

Music at events can raise licensing, copyright and local noise issues. A venue may already have cover or permissions, but do not assume. Ask what is included and whether any live music, DJ, recorded music or amplified sound creates extra requirements.

For public music licensing, check TheMusicLicence. For alcohol and wider licensing, check GOV.UK alcohol licensing guidance.

Your early legal checklist

  • Ask the venue what permissions are already in place.
  • Confirm who is responsible for licence applications or notices.
  • Check alcohol, music, food, noise, parking and public liability requirements.
  • Keep written confirmation from suppliers and the venue.
  • Do not leave council checks until the final weeks.
