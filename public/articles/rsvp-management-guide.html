RSVP Management Guide: Replies, Dietary Needs and Plus-Ones

RSVPs are not just a yes-or-no list. They drive catering, seating, supplier numbers, accessibility arrangements and your final cost control.

EventFlow Team Published: 14 June 2026 7 min read Beginner

The short version

  • Ask every guest whether they're attending, whether they have a dietary requirement, whether they need accessibility support and whether a plus-one or child place is being used — before suppliers start asking for final numbers.
  • Keep one single, clean source of truth for the guest list, with invited, accepted, declined and chasing tracked as separate, clearly labelled statuses.
  • Set your RSVP deadline earlier than your supplier deadline so you have time to chase calmly rather than urgently.
  • Before you submit final numbers to caterers, venues, stationers or hire companies, double-check plus-ones, children, allergies, evening guests and supplier meals — changes after that point can cost money.

Ask the Right Questions First

Collect all the details you will need before suppliers start asking for final numbers. At a minimum, ask each guest:

  • Whether they're attending
  • Whether they have a dietary requirement
  • Whether they need accessibility support
  • Whether any approved plus-one or child place is being used

Keep questions short. Guests are more likely to respond quickly when the form is clear and mobile friendly.

Keep the Guest List Clean

A clean RSVP list has one record per invited person, consistent names and clear status labels. Avoid keeping one list in messages, another in a spreadsheet and another in your head. That is how duplicates, missed meals and awkward table-plan issues happen.

  • Use one source of truth.
  • Mark invited, accepted, declined and chasing separately.
  • Keep notes short and factual.
  • Review the list before each supplier deadline.

Chase Without Making It Awkward

Set the RSVP deadline earlier than your supplier deadline. That gives you time to chase calmly, not urgently.

A short, friendly message usually works best. Remind them why you need the response and give a clear deadline.

Lock Numbers Before Supplier Deadlines

Caterers, venues, stationers and hire companies all work from final numbers. Once those figures are submitted, changes can cost money.

Final checks before you submit numbers

  • Plus-ones
  • Children
  • Allergies
  • Evening guests
  • Supplier meals
