What not to say

  • Do not promise guaranteed bookings, enquiries, revenue or paid subscriptions.
  • Do not say you work for EventFlow unless you have written approval.
  • Do not post in groups where supplier promotion or referral links are not allowed.
  • Do not encourage fake, duplicated or incomplete supplier accounts.
  • Do not state reward values that are not visible in your dashboard or campaign brief.
