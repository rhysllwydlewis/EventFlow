Getting started

What is EventFlow?

EventFlow is an all-in-one event planning platform for the UK. You can discover and save venues and suppliers, build structured event plans, track your budget, message suppliers directly, manage your guest list and RSVPs, and keep everything in one place — for free.

Is EventFlow free for customers?

Yes. Creating an account and using the core planning tools — event plans, budget tracker, supplier search, messaging, saved suppliers, and the wedding website — is completely free for customers.

What types of events can I plan on EventFlow?

EventFlow supports weddings, birthday parties, corporate events, anniversary celebrations, christenings, charity galas, and most other UK events that involve hiring suppliers — venues, photographers, caterers, florists, DJs, and more.

Do I need an account to browse suppliers?

You can browse the supplier directory without an account. To save suppliers, send messages, create an event plan, or use the budget tracker, you will need to sign up for a free EventFlow account.

Planning your event

How do I create an event plan?

After signing in, click Plan in the top navigation, or select Create a plan from your dashboard. The plan wizard walks you through setting your event date, guest count, and budget, then generates a personalised checklist and planning timeline.

How does the budget tracker work?

The budget tracker lets you set a total budget and log spending as you confirm suppliers. Your dashboard shows your total spent, remaining balance, and a real-time breakdown by category. You can update the budget at any time from Budget Settings on your dashboard.

What is the Wedding Website and RSVP feature?

EventFlow includes a free mini wedding website builder. You can create a personalised page with your event details, share it with guests, collect RSVPs online, manage your guest list, and build a seating plan — all from your dashboard under Wedding Website & RSVPs.

What are the planning checklists and upcoming tasks?

EventFlow generates a personalised planning checklist based on your event type and date. Each checklist item links directly to the relevant part of the platform. The Upcoming Tasks widget on your dashboard highlights what needs attention soon.

What is the Events Calendar?

The public Events Calendar shows upcoming events listed on EventFlow in the UK. Your personal event date is also visible in your dashboard calendar alongside key planning milestones.

Finding & working with suppliers

How do I find and compare suppliers?

Go to Suppliers and use the search filters to narrow by category, location, and price range. Each profile shows photos, a description, pricing, reviews, and available packages. Save suppliers you like and compare them from your dashboard.

How do I save suppliers to my shortlist?

On any supplier profile page, click the Save button. Saved suppliers appear in the Saved Suppliers section of your dashboard where you can add notes, compare options, and send enquiries directly.

How do enquiries and messages work?

When you contact a supplier, EventFlow creates a private message thread between you. View and reply from your dashboard under Conversations. Opt in to email notifications so you are alerted as soon as a supplier replies.

How do supplier reviews work?

After your event, you can leave a star rating and written review on any supplier you worked with. Reviews appear on their public profile and help other customers make informed choices. Suppliers cannot remove genuine reviews.

What is the Marketplace?

The Marketplace lets you browse specific packages offered by suppliers — for example a photography package that covers eight hours, edited photos, and an online gallery. Each package has a fixed price so you know exactly what you're getting before you enquire.

For suppliers

How do suppliers get listed on EventFlow?

Suppliers create a free account, complete a profile with photos, a service description, location, and pricing, then submit it for review. Once approved by an admin, the profile appears in search results and the supplier can start receiving enquiries.

What subscription plans are available for suppliers?

EventFlow offers a free tier and paid subscription plans for suppliers. Paid plans include higher search placement, unlimited photo uploads, featured placement in relevant searches, and advanced analytics. Full details are on the Pricing page.

How do I create packages on my supplier profile?

From your supplier dashboard, go to Packages and click Add a package. Set a title, description, price, and what's included. Packages appear on your profile and in the Marketplace for customers to browse and enquire about.

What is the EventFlow Partner Programme?

The Partner Programme lets anyone earn reward points by referring event suppliers to EventFlow. You receive a unique referral link and earn points each time a referred supplier signs up and gets active on the platform. Points can be cashed out as Amazon Vouchers or Pre-Paid Debit Cards once you reach £15. Sign up free here.

Account & settings

How do I reset my password?

On the login page, click Forgot password? and enter your registered email address. You will receive a reset link within a few minutes — it expires after one hour. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive, or email us at hello@event-flow.co.uk.

Can I change my notification preferences?

Yes. After signing in, go to Settings from your dashboard and toggle email notifications on or off. You can change this at any time.

How do I delete my account?

Email hello@event-flow.co.uk with the subject Account Deletion Request. Under UK GDPR you have the right to erasure of your personal data. We will acknowledge within 72 hours and confirm once your data has been removed.

Is my data safe with EventFlow?

Yes. EventFlow is operated by VEXI and complies with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. We use encrypted HTTPS connections, secure password hashing, and never sell your personal data. Read our Privacy Notice or visit the Legal Hub for full details.

Who do I contact if something isn't working?

Use the Contact page or email hello@event-flow.co.uk with a short description of the issue. If you are signed in, you can also raise a support ticket directly from your dashboard under Support.
