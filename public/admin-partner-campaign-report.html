Manual cashout review checklist

  • Check supplier quality: supplier name, company, location, category, package/profile activity and whether the profile appears genuine.
  • Check campaign pattern: unusually high volume, repeated source names, duplicate supplier details or suspicious incomplete profiles should be reviewed before approval.
  • Keep cashout manual: approve only after checking the partner, referral quality, available points and campaign context.
  • Use audit notes: manual bonuses and cashout approvals/rejections should record the reason clearly.
