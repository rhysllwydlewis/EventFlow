Your Data Rights

Last materially updated: {{POLICY_DATA_RIGHTS_LAST_UPDATED}} · Last reviewed: {{POLICY_DATA_RIGHTS_LAST_REVIEWED}} · Version: {{POLICY_DATA_RIGHTS_VERSION}}

At EventFlow, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have important rights regarding your personal information.

This page provides a user-friendly explanation of your rights and how to exercise them. For complete details, please refer to our Privacy Notice.

Your Rights at a Glance

📋 Right to Access

What it means: You can request a copy of all the personal data we hold about you.

How to exercise: Email {{ADMIN_EMAIL}} with the subject line "Data Access Request"

What you'll receive: A complete export of your data in JSON or CSV format

Response time: Without undue delay and normally within one calendar month

✏️ Right to Rectification

What it means: You can correct inaccurate or incomplete information about you.

How to exercise:

  • Self-Service: Update most information directly in your Settings page
  • Need help? Contact {{ADMIN_EMAIL}}

Examples: Correct your name, update your email address, fix business details

🗑️ Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)

What it means: You can request deletion of your personal data.

How to exercise: Email {{ADMIN_EMAIL}} with the subject line "Account Deletion Request"

What gets deleted:

  • Your user account and profile
  • Event plans and saved content
  • Messages and communications
  • Uploaded photos and media

Important notes:

  • Deleted account data may remain in a restricted recovery or backup state for a limited period before final deletion
  • We may retain certain data where required by law (e.g., audit logs for legal compliance)
  • Publicly visible reviews may be anonymized rather than deleted to maintain platform integrity

Response time: We will respond without undue delay and normally within one calendar month; completion may be limited by lawful retention requirements

📦 Right to Data Portability

What it means: You can receive your personal data in a machine-readable format and transfer it to another service.

How to exercise: Email {{ADMIN_EMAIL}} with the subject line "Data Portability Request"

What you'll receive: A structured JSON file containing:

  • Your profile information
  • Event plans and notes
  • Saved suppliers and preferences
  • Message history
  • Other user-generated content

Response time: Without undue delay and normally within one calendar month

🚫 Right to Object

What it means: You can object to certain ways we use your data.

Marketing emails:

  • Click "Unsubscribe" in any marketing email
  • Update preferences in your Settings
  • Email {{ADMIN_EMAIL}}

Note: You will still receive essential service emails (e.g., password resets, account verification)

⏸️ Right to Restrict Processing

What it means: You can ask us to temporarily stop processing your data in certain circumstances.

How to exercise: Email {{ADMIN_EMAIL}} with details of your request

When this applies:

  • You contest the accuracy of your data
  • Processing is unlawful but you don't want erasure
  • We no longer need the data but you need it for legal claims
  • You've objected to processing and we're verifying our legitimate grounds

How to Make a Request

To exercise any of your data rights:

  1. Email us: Send your request to {{ADMIN_EMAIL}}
  2. Include:
    • Your full name
    • Email address associated with your account
    • Clear description of your request
    • Any relevant account details to help us identify you
  3. Verification: We may ask for additional information to verify your identity before processing your request
  4. Response: We will respond without undue delay and normally within one calendar month, or notify you if a lawful extension applies

Response Times

We are committed to responding to your requests promptly:

  • Standard response: Without undue delay and normally within one calendar month of receiving your request or the information reasonably needed to verify identity
  • Extension: Where the law permits, a complex request or multiple requests may be extended by a further two months. We will explain the extension within the initial one-month period.
  • Free of charge: We will not charge a fee for most requests
  • Excessive requests: If your request is clearly unfounded or excessive, we may charge a reasonable fee or refuse the request

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I delete my account?

Email {{ADMIN_EMAIL}} with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will confirm your identity and handle the request within the applicable legal timeframe. Some data may remain for a limited recovery or backup period, or be retained where legally necessary, as explained in the Privacy Notice.

Q: Can I update my profile information myself?

Yes! Most profile information can be updated directly in your Settings page. Changes take effect immediately.

Q: What format will my data export be in?

Data exports are provided in JSON format (a standard machine-readable format). We can also provide CSV format for certain data types upon request.

Q: How long does it take to process a data request?

We respond without undue delay and normally within one calendar month. Where the law permits a longer period, we will notify you and explain why.

Q: Is there a fee for data requests?

No, we do not charge a fee for reasonable data requests. However, we reserve the right to charge a reasonable fee or refuse clearly unfounded or excessive requests.

Q: What if I'm not satisfied with your response?

If you're not satisfied with how we've handled your request, you can lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection supervisory authority. Visit ico.org.uk or call 0303 123 1113.
