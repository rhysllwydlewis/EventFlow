About EventFlow

Event planning made simple.

EventFlow is a UK event planning platform connecting customers with approved event suppliers for weddings, parties and corporate events. Alongside the supplier marketplace, we built planning tools for budgets, guest lists and timelines to help people organise an event in one place, rather than juggling spreadsheets and group chats across several other tools.

Who runs EventFlow

EventFlow is operated by VEXI. For support, partnership enquiries or general questions, see our contact page or email admin@event-flow.co.uk.

How our guides are written

The guides published under /guides are written and maintained by the EventFlow team. We draw on the practical questions we see from people planning real events through the platform — what they ask suppliers, where bookings go wrong, and what they wish they'd known earlier. Where a guide states a specific legal, tax or licensing figure, we link to the official government source for it rather than asking you to take our word for it, and we review guides periodically to keep dates and figures current.

We don't publish guides under invented personal bylines. Where a guide doesn't carry a named author, it reflects the EventFlow team's collective editorial work rather than one individual's.
