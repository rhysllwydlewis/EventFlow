    Questions

    Frequently asked questions

    Do you take commission on bookings?

    No. Your subscription is the only EventFlow platform charge, so the revenue from your bookings remains yours.

    Can I change plan later?

    Yes. You can upgrade as you grow or move to a lower plan when your needs change.

    What happens if I cancel?

    Your paid access continues until the end of the current billing period, then your account returns to Starter.

    Is EventFlow free for customers?

    Yes. Customers can plan events, find suppliers and send enquiries without paying a subscription.
