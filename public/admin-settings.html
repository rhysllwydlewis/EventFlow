Email Automation — Supplier Action Prompts

Automatically remind suppliers of outstanding actions (missing packages, incomplete profile, missing photos). Only suppliers with a verified email address and the setting enabled will receive these emails. The system is disabled by default to prevent accidental bulk sends.
