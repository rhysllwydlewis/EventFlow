Wedding website and RSVP questions

Is the EventFlow wedding website free?

Yes. The wedding website, RSVPs, guest list and seating plan are free for customers.

Do guests need an account to RSVP?

No. Guests open your link and reply on any phone, tablet or computer.

Can I keep my wedding website private?

Yes. Use a private link or a password. Wedding websites are hidden from search engines by default.

How does the wedding seating plan work?

Add tables, then drag attending guests to seats or auto-seat them. Only guests who are attending hold a seat.
