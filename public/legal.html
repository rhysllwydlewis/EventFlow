🍪 Cookie Policy

Last materially updated: {{POLICY_COOKIE_POLICY_LAST_UPDATED}} | Last reviewed: {{POLICY_COOKIE_POLICY_LAST_REVIEWED}} | Version: {{POLICY_COOKIE_POLICY_VERSION}}

EventFlow uses cookies to provide and improve our services.

Cookie Categories

Essential Cookies (Always Active)

  • Authentication Token: Keeps you logged in (7 days)
  • Cookie Consent: Remembers your cookie preferences (1 year)

Functional Cookies (With Your Consent)

  • Theme Preference: Remembers dark/light mode (localStorage)
  • UI Settings: Saves interface preferences such as expanded folders and notification settings (localStorage)

If you reject or withdraw functional consent, all functional preferences are removed from your browser immediately.

Analytics Cookies (With Your Consent)

  • EventFlow analytics: Records privacy-minimised page, journey, conversion and performance events. First-party analytics events are normally retained for 90 days, subject to deployment configuration.
  • PostHog: May record consented page views, attribution and masked session replays on non-sensitive pages. Form inputs are masked, query strings are removed from captured URLs and recording is blocked on areas such as messages, payments, dashboards and settings.
  • Attribution storage: A first-touch and last-touch acquisition record may be stored in localStorage for up to 90 days. An opaque analytics session identifier may be stored for the browser session.

Analytics does not start unless you consent. Withdrawing consent stops further analytics collection and removes EventFlow's browser attribution record. PostHog may use cookies or similar browser storage as part of the consented service.

Advertising Cookies (With Your Consent)

  • Google Ads conversion tag: Where you consent to advertising, EventFlow loads the Google Ads tag (gtag.js) from Google so we can measure which adverts lead to sign-ups and enquiries. Google may set cookies or use similar browser storage for advertising and conversion measurement, and receives ordinary web-request data such as an IP address, browser information and the page you were on.

Advertising is a separate choice from analytics: consenting to analytics does not switch advertising on. The tag is not loaded on admin pages, and nothing is requested from Google until you consent. When the tag does load, EventFlow first declares Google Consent Mode defaults of denied for advertising storage, advertising user data and ad personalisation, and only then records your consent. If you later withdraw advertising consent, those signals are set back to denied for the rest of the session and the tag is not loaded again on your next visit.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You can manage or withdraw your consent at any time — not just on your first visit. Use the button below or the Cookie preferences link in the site footer.

You can also to be prompted again from scratch.

  • Accept All: Allow every optional category — functional, analytics and advertising
  • Reject: Only essential cookies — functional preferences are cleared, and analytics and advertising do not start
  • Manage Preferences: Choose functional, analytics and advertising separately
  • Browser Settings: Control cookies via your browser

Third-party services: If you consent to analytics, PostHog processes analytics data for us. If you consent to advertising, Google receives conversion-measurement data through the Google Ads tag. Ordinary web requests to those providers may disclose technical data such as an IP address and browser information even though EventFlow's own behaviour-event store excludes raw IP addresses and raw user-agent strings.

This notice is intended to explain our use of cookies and similar technologies. It should be reviewed against the live production configuration and is not a substitute for legal advice.

📍 Unclaimed Listings

Last materially updated: {{POLICY_UNCLAIMED_LISTINGS_LAST_UPDATED}} | Last reviewed: {{POLICY_UNCLAIMED_LISTINGS_LAST_REVIEWED}} | Version: {{POLICY_UNCLAIMED_LISTINGS_VERSION}}

Some supplier listings on EventFlow are created by us from information a business has already published about itself, rather than by the business signing up. Those listings are labelled Unclaimed wherever they appear.

Where the information comes from

  • The business's own public website, including the business name, description, category, service area, a publicly listed phone number or email address, and images published on that site
  • Public directory-style information such as a town, region or postcode used to place the listing on a UK location page

We do not use information from a private or access-controlled source, and we do not collect special category data for these listings.

What an unclaimed listing is and is not

  • It is not an endorsement, recommendation or verification of the business by EventFlow
  • It does not mean the business is an EventFlow customer, subscriber or partner, and no charge arises from it
  • It does not create a contract between EventFlow and the business
  • Information may be out of date, because it reflects what was published at the time it was collected

Claiming your listing

If the listing is yours, use the Claim this listing link on the profile. We look for signals that your account belongs to the business — an email address on the business's own domain is the usual one — and the request then goes to a proof stage before the listing is handed over. Signing up with a personal webmail address does not on its own establish the connection, so we may ask for further evidence. Once the claim is accepted you own the listing and can correct or complete it yourself.

Correction, objection and removal

You do not have to claim a listing in order to have it corrected or taken down. Email {{ADMIN_EMAIL}} with the subject "Unclaimed listing" and the listing URL, and tell us whether you want it corrected or removed. We will:

  • act on a removal request for a listing we created, without asking you to justify it;
  • correct information you tell us is wrong; and
  • respond without undue delay and normally within one calendar month, as set out under Data Protection & Your Rights.

Where the listing includes personal data — for example a sole trader's trading name or a named contact — we rely on our legitimate interests in operating a UK supplier directory, and you have the right to object to that processing. The Privacy Notice explains the sourcing, lawful basis and retention in full.

If an image on an unclaimed listing is yours and you would rather it were not shown, tell us and we will remove it. See Copyright & Intellectual Property for the general notice route.

🛒 Marketplace Terms

Last materially updated: {{POLICY_MARKETPLACE_TERMS_LAST_UPDATED}} | Last reviewed: {{POLICY_MARKETPLACE_TERMS_LAST_REVIEWED}} | Version: {{POLICY_MARKETPLACE_TERMS_VERSION}}

The EventFlow Marketplace is a peer-to-peer platform for buying and selling physical event-related items. These terms apply to all marketplace users (buyers and sellers).

Marketplace Overview

  • Physical Items Only: The marketplace is for tangible event items (decorations, furniture, equipment, supplies)
  • No Services: Event services should be booked through supplier listings, not the marketplace
  • Peer-to-Peer: Transactions occur directly between buyers and sellers
  • Platform Role: EventFlow facilitates connections but is not party to transactions
  • UK Focus: Marketplace primarily serves UK-based users

Seller Requirements & Obligations

Accurate Listings:

  • Provide truthful descriptions of item condition, size, and functionality
  • Upload clear, genuine photos showing actual item (not stock photos)
  • Set fair pricing based on item condition and market value
  • Specify accurate location for pickup or delivery
  • Update listings promptly when items are sold or unavailable

Legal Compliance:

  • You own the items you list or have permission to sell them
  • Items must be legal to sell in the UK
  • You have rights to all photos and content you upload
  • You comply with consumer protection and safety regulations
  • You handle any applicable VAT or tax obligations

Communication & Transaction:

  • Respond to buyer inquiries within 48 hours
  • Arrange secure payment methods (PayPal, bank transfer, cash on collection)
  • Arrange pickup/delivery logistics with buyer
  • Provide accurate availability for collection times
  • Handle transactions professionally and courteously

Buyer Guidelines & Responsibilities

Before Purchase:

  • Review item description, photos, and condition carefully
  • Ask questions about condition, measurements, or functionality
  • Verify seller location and collection/delivery arrangements
  • Agree on payment method and amount before committing
  • Check seller ratings and reviews if available

Payment & Collection:

  • Use secure payment methods (avoid cash payments for high-value items)
  • Inspect items in person before payment when collecting
  • Arrange safe collection times and public meeting places
  • Get proof of purchase/receipt for expensive items
  • Contact seller promptly if items don't match description

Prohibited Items & Conduct

Prohibited Items:

  • Counterfeit, replica, or knock-off items
  • Stolen or illegally obtained goods
  • Dangerous or hazardous materials
  • Restricted items (weapons, explosives, controlled substances)
  • Services (use supplier listings instead)
  • Items violating intellectual property rights
  • Perishable food items (safety concerns)

Prohibited Conduct:

  • Fake or misleading listings
  • Bait-and-switch tactics
  • Harassment or threatening behavior
  • Price manipulation or artificial scarcity
  • Attempting to circumvent platform policies
  • Identity theft or impersonation

Platform Role & Liability Disclaimer

EventFlow's Role:

  • We provide a platform for users to connect and transact
  • We do not verify item conditions or inspect listings
  • We are not party to buyer-seller transactions
  • We do not process payments or handle money transfers
  • We do not arrange delivery or logistics

Liability Disclaimer:

  • Not Responsible for Items: EventFlow is not responsible for item quality, condition, authenticity, or safety
  • Not Responsible for Transactions: We are not liable for payment disputes, delivery issues, or transaction failures
  • Not Responsible for Users: We do not guarantee user identity, reliability, or trustworthiness
  • No Warranties: Items are sold "as is" between users; EventFlow makes no warranties
  • User Responsibility: Users assume all risks when buying or selling through the marketplace

Dispute Resolution - Comprehensive Process

EventFlow provides a structured dispute resolution process for marketplace transactions:

Step 1: Direct Resolution Between Parties (48-Hour Response Time)

Always attempt to resolve issues directly first:

  • Contact the other party through EventFlow messaging system
  • Clearly state the problem and desired resolution
  • Provide photos or evidence to support your case
  • Allow 48 hours for initial response
  • Remain professional and courteous throughout communication
  • Document all communication for potential platform mediation

Step 2: Platform Mediation

If direct resolution fails after 48 hours or other party is unresponsive:

  • Contact: {{ABUSE_EMAIL}}
  • Subject Line: "Marketplace Dispute - [Order/Listing Number]"
  • Include in Your Report:
    • Listing URL and item details
    • Transaction amount and payment method
    • Complete timeline of events
    • Screenshots of all relevant messages
    • Photos of item (if applicable)
    • Explanation of issue and desired outcome
    • Evidence of attempts at direct resolution

Step 3: Platform Review

EventFlow will conduct a thorough investigation:

  • Acknowledgment: Sent when reasonably practicable after receiving a sufficiently detailed report
  • Evidence Review: Examination of all submitted documentation
  • Both Parties Contacted: Each party has opportunity to provide their account
  • Policy Review: Check if marketplace terms were violated
  • Review Timeline: Depends on complexity, evidence and whether the parties respond
  • Updates Provided: Status updates where appropriate and supported by the dispute process

Step 4: Possible Outcomes

Based on investigation findings, EventFlow may take the following actions:

For Policy Violations:

  • Listing Removal: Immediate removal of violating listings
  • Official Warning: Formal warning issued to user account
  • Temporary Suspension: 7-30 day suspension for moderate violations
  • Permanent Ban: Account termination for serious violations
  • Listing Privileges Revoked: Removal of marketplace selling privileges

For Good Faith Disputes (No Clear Violation):

  • Mediation Guidance: Suggestions for resolution
  • Communication Facilitation: Help parties reach agreement
  • External Resources: Direction to Citizens Advice or other services
  • No Platform Action: If both parties acted in good faith

EventFlow Cannot:

  • Force refunds or payments (we don't process transactions)
  • Compel item returns or exchanges
  • Award financial compensation
  • Make legally binding decisions on ownership or liability

Step 5: Escalation to Small Claims Court

If platform mediation doesn't resolve financial disputes:

  • Eligibility: Disputes under £10,000 (England/Wales)
  • Process: www.gov.uk/make-court-claim-for-money
  • Costs: Court fees typically £25-£455 based on claim value
  • Timeline: Usually 6-12 months to resolve
  • Legal Advice: Consider free initial consultation with solicitor

Step 6: Citizens Advice Consumer Service

For consumer rights advice and support:

  • Phone: 0808 223 1133 (Monday-Friday, 9am-5pm)
  • Website: www.citizensadvice.org.uk/consumer/
  • Services: Free advice on consumer rights, dispute resolution guidance
  • Trading Standards Referral: Can refer serious cases to Trading Standards

Step 7: Trading Standards

For serious violations or illegal trading:

  • Access: Via Citizens Advice Consumer Service
  • When to Report: Counterfeit goods, scams, dangerous products, persistent violations
  • Enforcement: Trading Standards has legal enforcement powers
  • Criminal Prosecution: Serious cases may lead to prosecution

Evidence Requirements for Disputes

To support your dispute claim, provide:

  • Transaction Evidence: Payment confirmations, receipts, invoices
  • Communication Records: All messages between parties (platform messages, emails)
  • Visual Evidence: Photos/videos of item condition, packaging, defects
  • Listing Documentation: Screenshots of original listing description and photos
  • Delivery Proof: Tracking numbers, delivery confirmations, signed receipts
  • Third-Party Reports: Professional assessments if relevant (e.g., authenticity certificates)

Timeframes for Dispute Resolution

Stage Timeframe
Direct party resolution attempt 48 hours for initial response
Platform dispute report acknowledgment As soon as reasonably practicable
Platform investigation Depends on complexity and evidence

Best Practice for Dispute Prevention:

  • Keep all communication on the EventFlow platform
  • Document everything with photos and screenshots
  • Be clear about item condition and transaction terms upfront
  • Use secure payment methods with buyer protection
  • Meet in public places for high-value item exchanges
  • Trust your instincts - if something seems wrong, don't proceed

Content Ownership & Licensing

Your Content:

  • You own all photos and descriptions you upload
  • You grant EventFlow a license to display your listings on the platform
  • This license is non-exclusive, worldwide, and royalty-free
  • The license continues while your listing is active
  • You can delete your listing at any time to revoke the license

Photo Requirements:

  • Use clear, well-lit photos showing the actual item

  • Photos must be your own (not stock images or competitors' photos)
  • Remove photos with personal information or location markers
  • Maximum file size: 10MB per photo
  • Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WEBP

Listing Removal Rights

EventFlow reserves the right to remove marketplace listings for:

  • Violations of these marketplace terms
  • Prohibited items or conduct
  • Customer complaints or reports
  • Suspected fraud or misrepresentation
  • Inactivity (listings over 90 days old may be archived)

Safety Tips

For Buyers:

  • Meet in public places for item collection
  • Bring someone with you for high-value items
  • Inspect items before payment
  • Use secure payment methods with buyer protection
  • Trust your instincts - if it seems too good to be true, it probably is

For Sellers:

  • Meet buyers in public, well-lit areas
  • Have someone with you when meeting buyers
  • Count cash payments carefully before handing over items
  • Keep records of all transactions
  • Don't share personal financial information

↩️ Marketplace Returns & Consumer Rights

Understanding the Marketplace Model

EventFlow operates a peer-to-peer marketplace where transactions occur directly between buyers and sellers. This structure affects returns and consumer rights:

Consumer Rights Act 2015

For Trader Sales (Business Sellers):

  • Full Consumer Rights Act 2015 protections apply
  • 14-day cooling off period for distance sales (Distance Selling Regulations)
  • Goods must be as described, fit for purpose, and of satisfactory quality
  • Right to reject faulty items within 30 days for full refund
  • Right to repair or replacement for faults within 6 months

For Private Sales (Individual Sellers):

  • Limited statutory rights compared to trader sales
  • Items must still match description
  • No automatic 14-day cooling off period
  • Returns subject to seller's individual return policy
  • Seller must clearly state if they are private or business seller

How to Request a Return

  1. Contact Seller First: Message the seller directly through the EventFlow platform within 48 hours of receiving the item
  2. State Your Reason: Clearly explain why you want to return (not as described, faulty, changed mind, etc.)
  3. Provide Evidence: Include photos if item is damaged or not as described
  4. Wait for Response: Seller should respond within 48 hours
  5. Arrange Return: If seller agrees, arrange return shipping (who pays depends on reason for return)
  6. Platform Mediation: If seller doesn't respond or refuses unfairly, contact {{ABUSE_EMAIL}}

EventFlow's Limited Role

As a platform intermediary, EventFlow:

  • Does NOT: Handle returns directly between buyers and sellers
  • Does NOT: Process refunds for marketplace transactions
  • Does NOT: Act as party to the transaction
  • Does: Provide platform for communication between parties
  • Does: Mediate disputes when direct resolution fails
  • Does: Remove listings that breach policies
  • Does: Suspend or ban users who violate marketplace rules

Who Pays Return Shipping?

  • Item Faulty/Not as Described: Seller should pay return shipping
  • Change of Mind (Private Sale): Buyer typically pays unless seller's policy states otherwise
  • Change of Mind (Trader Sale): Buyer pays return shipping for cooling off period returns
  • Wrong Item Sent: Seller pays return shipping

UK Consumer Rights Resources

For independent advice on your consumer rights:

  • Citizens Advice Consumer Service: 0808 223 1133
  • Website: www.citizensadvice.org.uk/consumer/
  • Which? Consumer Rights: www.which.co.uk/consumer-rights
  • Trading Standards: Contact via Citizens Advice Consumer Service

Important: Always check whether a seller is trading as a business or private individual. Business sellers must comply with full Consumer Rights Act 2015. If unclear, ask the seller directly or contact our support team.

💳 Payment Security

Current Payment Model

Important Information About Marketplace Payments:

  • EventFlow does NOT currently process payments directly for marketplace transactions
  • Payment arrangements are made peer-to-peer between buyers and sellers
  • We are NOT a party to financial transactions
  • We do NOT hold buyer or seller funds

Recommended Secure Payment Methods

We strongly recommend using payment methods with buyer protection:

✅ Recommended (Buyer Protection)

  • PayPal Goods & Services: Comprehensive buyer protection, dispute resolution
  • Credit Card: Section 75 protection for purchases £100-£30,000
  • Debit Card: Chargeback rights under payment schemes
  • Payment Platforms: Stripe, Square, or similar with buyer protection

⚠️ Use With Caution

  • Bank Transfer: Limited buyer protection - only use for trusted sellers or services
  • PayPal Friends & Family: No buyer protection - not recommended for marketplace purchases
  • Cash in Person: Acceptable for local pickups with proper safety precautions

❌ Never Use

  • Gift Cards: Common scam method, no protection
  • Cryptocurrency (for standard purchases): Irreversible, no buyer protection
  • Western Union / MoneyGram: Untraceable, favored by scammers
  • Unknown Payment Apps: Stick to recognized, established platforms

Safety Guidelines

  1. Never Pay Outside the Agreed Method: If seller requests a different payment method after agreement, be suspicious
  2. Document Everything: Keep records of all communications and payment confirmations
  3. Use Platform Messaging: Keep discussions on EventFlow for dispute evidence
  4. Verify Seller Identity: Check seller ratings, reviews, and verification status
  5. Be Cautious of Pressure: Scammers often create urgency ("pay now or lose it")
  6. Check PayPal Type: Ensure "Goods & Services" not "Friends & Family"

Cashout Requests

Partners may convert available credits into one of the following reward types:
