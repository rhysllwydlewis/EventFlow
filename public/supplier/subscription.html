Questions

Frequently asked questions

What happens during the free trial?

During your free trial, you'll have full access to all features of your chosen plan. You won't be charged until the trial period ends. You can cancel anytime during the trial with no charge.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades apply immediately with a pro-rated charge; downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period, so you keep what you've already paid for.

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel from this page at any time. You'll continue to have access to premium features until the end of your current billing period.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept payments through Stripe, which supports all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, and more.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. All payments are processed securely through Stripe, which uses industry-standard encryption and PCI-DSS Level 1 certification. We never store your payment information on our servers.
