# EventFlow Partner Cashout SOP

This SOP keeps partner cashout manual by design. Manual review is intentional: it allows EventFlow to verify that points were earned through genuine supplier referrals before any reward is issued.

## Purpose

- Protect EventFlow from fake, duplicated or low-quality referral activity.
- Make sure reward values are issued only after points have matured and passed review.
- Preserve a clear audit trail for every approval, rejection, processing step and delivery.
- Keep the partner reward experience professional without automating away the fraud check.

## Status workflow

1. `submitted`
   - Partner has requested a cashout.
   - Points are held so they cannot be reused while the request is reviewed.

2. `approved`
   - Admin has completed the fraud and quality checks and is happy for the reward to be prepared.

3. `processing`
   - Admin is buying/preparing the Amazon Voucher or Pre-Paid Debit Card.

4. `delivered`
   - Reward has been delivered to the partner.
   - The hold is finalised as a permanent redemption.

5. `rejected`
   - Request failed checks or cannot be fulfilled.
   - Held points are released back to the partner.

## Manual review checklist

Before approving a cashout request, the admin must check:

- Partner account is active and not flagged.
- Partner has not referred themselves unless explicitly approved.
- Referred suppliers appear genuine event businesses.
- Supplier accounts are not obvious duplicates, spam, tests or placeholder accounts.
- Supplier businesses have enough information to be considered legitimate, such as business name, service category, location, and ideally a profile/package/photo trail.
- Points being cashed out are available, not maturing, and not already held against another request.
- Any campaign bonus has an audit note explaining the basis of the award.
- Campaign/source metadata looks reasonable where present.
- No unusual pattern is present, such as many suppliers from the same email format, IP pattern, repeated company names, or incomplete profiles.

## Approval guidance

Approve where the request is legitimate and there are no obvious abuse concerns.

Reject where there is evidence or reasonable suspicion of:

- fake suppliers
- duplicate suppliers
- self-referral without approval
- spam/referral abuse
- bot/test activity
- misleading partner promotion
- points generated by error
- reward request made before operational checks are complete

When rejecting, add a partner-visible response where appropriate and an internal note explaining the reason.

## Delivery evidence

When marking a request as delivered, record a delivery reference. This should not expose unnecessary sensitive information.

Good examples:

- `Amazon voucher issued via admin@email.co.uk, order ending 1234`
- `Pre-paid card reference ending 5678`
- `Voucher sent to partner email on 11/06/2026`

Avoid storing full voucher codes unless there is a specific operational need. Where possible, store a masked reference and keep the full code in the reward provider/account record.

## Partner response message examples

Approved:

> Thanks, your cashout request has passed review and is now approved. We will prepare the reward manually and update the status once it is being processed.

Processing:

> Your reward is now being prepared. We normally complete manual delivery within 3 to 5 working days.

Delivered:

> Your reward has now been delivered. Please check your email and let us know if anything has not arrived as expected.

Rejected:

> We have reviewed this request and cannot approve it at this stage. Any held points have been released where applicable. Please contact us if you believe this needs reviewing.

## Internal note examples

- `Approved after reviewing 12 referred suppliers. No duplicate/self-referral concerns found.`
- `Rejected. Multiple referred suppliers appear incomplete/test accounts and do not meet quality threshold.`
- `Delivered Amazon voucher. Reference masked in delivery details. Full record retained in provider account.`
- `Manual campaign bonus checked against Founding Partner June offer and approved.`

## Launch bonus handling

For founding partner campaigns, use manual credit adjustments with clear audit notes. Suggested audit note format:

`Founding Partner Campaign Bonus: [campaign name], [qualification], [admin initials], [date]`

Examples:

- `Founding Partner Campaign Bonus: June supplier push, 10 approved supplier profiles, RL, 11/06/2026`
- `Quality Bonus: 5 referred suppliers with complete profiles/packages/photos, RL, 11/06/2026`

## Minimum operational standard before wider rollout

- Manual cashout SOP visible to admins.
- Admin cashout screen makes the review checklist clear.
- Delivery reference required when marking delivered.
- Internal note required when approving, rejecting or delivering.
- Campaign reporting visible to admins.
- Founding partner campaign bonus rules documented before recruitment.
